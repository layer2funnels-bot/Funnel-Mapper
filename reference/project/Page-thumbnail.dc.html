Where the thumbnail travels: the captured image renders at every zoom on the canvas (cropped to the 48px strip, full on hover), on the selected-state card, in the library's recents, in Presentation stages and in PDF / PNG / SVG exports.  ·  Fallback is the design, not a stopgap: with no URL the block shows its semantic page-type wireframe, so a map is fully readable before any page is built.  ·  Capture is display-only — it never touches funnel maths or the data model.
